# Morning brief

Default scope: today, the last 7 days, and anything overdue. Honour an
explicit scope if one is given ("brief for this month").

## Steps

1. **Reminders and commitments.** Read `memory/notes/reminders.md` and
   `memory/notes/commitments.md`: overdue first, then due in scope, then
   undated open items. Call out *waiting-on* items aged 7 days or more as
   nudge candidates, and anything the principal owes someone they are
   meeting soon.
2. **Time-sensitive people and project items.** Scan dossiers and project
   pages for dated entries landing in scope — goal checkpoints, review
   dates, anniversaries such as hire dates, and follow-ups marked `⚠`.
3. **Recent activity.** From `memory/log.md`, what entered the vault in
   scope.
4. **Loose ends.** Unprocessed inbox files, `⚠ unconfirmed` entries, and
   unanswered open questions from recent meetings.
5. Deliver in chat, tight and scannable, under the headings
   `Overdue / Today / This week / FYI`.

Produce no file output unless asked. If a written brief is wanted, follow the
report rules in `rules/shared/40-reports.md` — `brief` is a natural template
to evolve in `reports/templates/`.
