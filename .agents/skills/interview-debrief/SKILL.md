# Interview debrief

Interviews are constant for a senior lead, and raw impressions decay in
hours. Capture them structured, keep them calibratable, handle them as the
most sensitive data in the vault.

## Recording a debrief

1. **File**: `memory/people/candidate-{{firstname-lastname}}.md` — the
   `candidate-` prefix keeps candidates distinct from colleagues in the
   people index. First debrief creates it with: role + level interviewed
   for, req/pipeline name, recruiter, stage.
2. **Rubric**: on first use, ask for the principal's company competency
   rubric (or leveling axes) and record it in
   `memory/topics/interview-rubric.md`; every later debrief scores against
   it, so feedback is comparable across candidates.
3. **Per interview, capture** (dated):
   - Signals per competency — verdict + the **specific evidence** (what
     the candidate said/did), not adjectives. Evidence is what survives
     a hiring-committee challenge.
   - Level assessment vs the target level, with the strongest
     counter-example each way.
   - Recommendation on the principal's scale (record the scale in the
     rubric page too).
   - Open questions for later stages to probe.
4. **Never blend candidates** in one page; comparisons happen at decision
   time, not capture time.

## Decision support

- "Compare candidates for X" → table per competency from recorded
  evidence only; flag where evidence is thin rather than inferring.
- "Prep me to interview Y" → their page (if prior stages exist), the open
  probe questions, and which competencies are still uncovered for the loop.

## Handling rules

- This is HR-sensitive data: never surface candidate details in any other
  context (reports, briefs, other dossiers) — exceed the vault's normal
  discretion rules.
- On the principal's word that a pipeline closed, offer to archive the
  candidate pages (move content into a single dated closure note per the
  principal's company retention preference — ask once, record it in the
  rubric page).
