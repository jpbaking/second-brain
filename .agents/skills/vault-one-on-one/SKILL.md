# 1:1 prep and capture

A recurring 1:1 with a report, peer, or the principal's own manager. Two
modes: prep before, capture after. Default to prep unless the principal is
clearly recounting a conversation that already happened.

## Prep — work from the person named

1. Read their dossier fully, plus their entries in
   `memory/notes/commitments.md`, recent meeting pages, and their goals
   section.
2. Deliver talking points in chat:
   - **Recognise** — recent accomplishments not yet acknowledged.
   - **Follow up** — their open commitments and how overdue they are, and the
     principal's open commitments *to them*. Never let the principal walk in
     owing something unmentioned.
   - **Probe** — recorded concerns, attrition signals, and stale goals, each
     with one suggested open question.
   - **Goal pulse** — where each goal stands against its checkpoints. This is
     what makes eval season painless.
   - **Carry-over** — unresolved items from the last 1:1.
3. For a 1:1 with the principal's own manager, invert the lens: status to
   report upward, asks and escalations, and decisions needing air cover.

## Capture — work from what happened

4. File the notes to the person's **Interaction log** as a dated entry,
   update their goals, concerns, and accomplishments sections, mirror new
   promises into `memory/notes/commitments.md`, and update the indexes and
   `memory/log.md` per the capture rules in `rules/shared/20-capture.md`.
   Confirm in two lines.
