# Workflow: one-on-one (1:1 prep and capture)

Recurring 1:1 with a report, peer, or the principal's own manager.
Two modes — prep (before) and capture (after).

## Prep (default): input = the person

1. Read their dossier fully, plus their entries in commitments, recent
   meeting pages, and the goals section.
2. Deliver talking points in chat:
   - **Recognise** — recent accomplishments not yet acknowledged.
   - **Follow up** — their open commitments and how overdue; the
     principal's open commitments *to them* (never walk in owing
     something unmentioned).
   - **Probe** — recorded concerns, attrition signals, stale goals;
     one suggested open question each.
   - **Goal pulse** — where each goal stands vs its checkpoints (this is
     what makes eval season painless).
   - **Carry-over** — unresolved items from the last 1:1.
3. For a 1:1 with the principal's own manager, invert the lens: status
   to report upward, asks/escalations, decisions needing air cover.

## Capture (after): input = what happened

4. File the notes to the person's **Interaction log** (dated), update
   goals/concerns/accomplishments sections, mirror new promises into
   `notes/commitments.md`, and update indexes + log per the capture
   rules. Confirm in two lines.
