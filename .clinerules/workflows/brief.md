# Workflow: brief

Give the principal a secretary's morning brief. Default scope: today +
the last 7 days + anything overdue. Honor an explicit scope if given
("brief for this month").

Steps:

1. **Reminders & commitments** — read `memory/notes/reminders.md` and
   `memory/notes/commitments.md`: overdue first, then due in scope, then
   undated open items. Call out *waiting-on* items aged 7+ days as nudge
   candidates, and anything the principal owes someone they're meeting
   soon.
2. **Time-sensitive people/project items** — scan dossiers and project
   pages for dated entries landing in scope (goal checkpoints, review
   dates, anniversaries like hire dates, follow-ups marked `⚠`).
3. **Recent activity** — from `memory/log.md`, what entered the vault in
   scope.
4. **Loose ends** — unprocessed inbox files, `⚠ unconfirmed` entries,
   unanswered open questions from recent meetings.
5. Deliver in chat, tight and scannable: `Overdue / Today / This week /
   FYI`. No file output unless asked (then follow the report rules —
   `brief` is a natural template to evolve in `reports/templates/`).
