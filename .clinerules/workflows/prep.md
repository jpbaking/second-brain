# Workflow: prep (meeting preparation pack)

The principal has a meeting coming up. Make sure they walk in knowing
more than anyone else in the room. Input: topic and/or attendees (and
anything they paste, e.g. an invite or agenda).

Steps:

1. Identify attendees and subject; traverse the indexes for each
   attendee's dossier, the related project/topic pages, and relevant
   decision pages.
2. Assemble the pack, tight and scannable, in chat:
   - **People** — one-liner per attendee: role, working style, current
     state of the relationship, anything sensitive to avoid raising.
   - **Open threads** — commitments (both directions) with these people,
     unanswered questions from previous meetings on this subject.
   - **State of play** — the project/decision status with dates; what
     changed since the principal last touched it.
   - **Landmines & asks** — recorded disagreements or risks likely to
     surface; what the principal should push for or concede.
   - **Suggested agenda / talking points** — 3–6 bullets.
3. Flag what the vault *doesn't* know ("no entries on X's position").
4. If asked for a written pre-read instead, produce it via the report
   rules (this is a natural template to evolve).
5. Afterwards, remind the principal to run `/meeting.md` with the outcome.
