# Workflow: handoff (out-of-office / delegation brief)

The principal will be away (leave, travel, offsite). Produce the document
that lets their stand-in act — and the re-entry brief when they return.

## Before (input: dates, stand-in if known)

1. Gather everything dated within the window: reminders, commitments
   (both directions), goal checkpoints, decision revisit dates, meeting
   cadences (1:1s that will be missed).
2. Produce a handoff document via the report rules (document shape):
   - **Delegations** — items someone else must move, each with owner,
     context link, and what "done" looks like.
   - **Can wait** — items safely parked, with new dates.
   - **Watch-outs** — running risks, people situations the stand-in
     should handle with care (respect discretion rules — share the
     minimum the stand-in needs).
   - **Do-not-decide list** — decisions that must wait for the principal.
3. Re-date parked reminders; mark delegated commitments with the
   delegate's name. Log it.

## After ("I'm back")

4. Deliver a re-entry brief: what changed in the vault during the window,
   what came due, what the principal owes people who covered.
